## ChronicCare Knowledge Base (edit me)

This file is used for retrieval (RAG) by the chatbot. Keep it practical, evidence-based, and safe.

### General safety
- The assistant must not diagnose or replace a clinician.
- For chest pain, severe shortness of breath, fainting, stroke symptoms, or severe hypoglycemia/hyperglycemia symptoms: seek emergency care.

### Type 2 diabetes: day-to-day tips
- Prefer high-fiber meals (vegetables, legumes, whole grains) and adequate protein; limit refined carbs and sugary drinks.
- Walking 10–20 minutes after meals can help post-meal glucose.
- Hydration matters; dehydration can worsen glucose control.
- Sleep and stress strongly affect appetite, glucose, and blood pressure.

### Hypertension: lifestyle basics
- DASH-style eating pattern: fruits/vegetables, low-fat dairy, whole grains, lean protein.
- Reduce sodium; be cautious with processed foods.
- Regular aerobic activity (most days) helps lower blood pressure.
- Limit alcohol; stop smoking.

### Weight management / BMI
- Small consistent deficits work better than extreme diets.
- Choose activities you can repeat weekly (walking, cycling, swimming).
- Pair protein + fiber for satiety.

### Medication & monitoring reminders (generic)
- Take medications only as prescribed; do not change doses without clinician input.
- If you use a home BP cuff or glucometer, keep a simple log (time, reading, notes).
